Faith & Purpose

The Meaning Behind "El-Goshen"

The name El-Goshen reflects both faith and purpose, embodying our mission to be a beacon of restoration and dignity.

"EL" (Hebrew)

Refers to God, symbolizing strength, sovereignty, and divine guidance in everything we do.

"GOSHEN"

Represents a place of refuge, preservation, provision, and flourishing even during challenging times.

Inspired by faith, we serve everyone without discrimination.

Building resilient, healthy, inclusive, and sustainable communities.

Strategic Framework

Our 5 Strategic Pillars

How we ensure meaningful, measurable, and sustainable social impact.

1. Educate

Deliver evidence-based health & social awareness across communities and schools.

2. Empower

Equip women, youth, and vulnerable groups with skills, confidence, and tools.

3. Engage

Collaborate with healthcare professionals, faith leaders, schools, and government.

4. Innovate

Embrace creative, digital, and research-backed approaches to social challenges.

5. Sustain

Build annual, long-term programs that foster community ownership and resilience.
