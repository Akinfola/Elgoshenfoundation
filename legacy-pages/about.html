About Us

Building Hope. Empowering Lives. Transforming Communities.

El-Goshen Foundation is a non-profit, non-governmental organization committed to improving lives through health promotion, education, empowerment, disability inclusion, humanitarian action, environmental sustainability, and community development.

Founded on the belief that every individual deserves dignity, opportunity, and hope, the Foundation develops practical, people-centered solutions that address challenges faced by vulnerable populations while promoting sustainable development.

Our Story

Every Vision Begins With a Reason

El-Goshen Foundation was born from a deeply personal experience that transformed pain into purpose.

After experiencing recurring breast lumps over several years and facing the emotional and physical challenges that accompanied them, our Founder, Esther Kukoyi, entered a season of prayer, reflection, and deep gratitude. During that period, she became acutely aware that while she had access to medical evaluation and support, many women facing similar health concerns did not.

This realization inspired a vision to educate women and girls about breast health, encourage early detection, reduce stigma, and support vulnerable individuals who often lack access to accurate information and healthcare resources.

As the vision matured, it became evident that the needs of communities extend beyond healthcare alone. Poverty, education, disability, environmental challenges, and limited opportunities are deeply interconnected. Today, El-Goshen Foundation serves as a platform for hope, empowerment, and sustainable community transformation through integrated programmes.

Faith & Purpose

While inspired by faith, El-Goshen Foundation serves all people without discrimination and welcomes partnerships with individuals and organizations who share our commitment to improving lives.

Etymology & Identity

The Meaning of "El-Goshen"

"EL"

Hebrew word used in Scripture referring to God, symbolizing strength, sovereignty, and divine presence.

"GOSHEN"

Represents a place of refuge, preservation, provision, and hope—where people find protection and flourish even during difficult times.

Governance & Leadership

Our Founder & Governance

Esther Kukoyi

Founder

Leadership Through Purpose & Compassion

Esther Kukoyi is the Founder of El-Goshen Foundation. She is passionate about community development, health promotion, climate action, disability inclusion, and sustainable development.

Inspired by her personal experience with recurring breast lumps and guided by a desire to transform gratitude into service, she established El-Goshen Foundation to improve lives through education, empowerment, advocacy, and compassionate action.

She believes that lasting change begins when individuals and communities are equipped with knowledge, opportunities, and hope.

Governance Framework

El-Goshen Foundation is governed by a Constitution and Governance Handbook and led by a Board of Trustees responsible for strategic oversight, ethical leadership, transparency, accountability, and long-term sustainability.
