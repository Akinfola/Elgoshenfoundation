Founder

Esther Kukoyi

Founder & Visionary Leader

Esther Kukoyi is passionate about community development, health promotion, climate action, disability inclusion, and sustainable development.

Inspired by her personal journey of overcoming recurring breast health challenges, she established El-Goshen Foundation to transform gratitude into service. She believes that lasting change begins when individuals and communities are equipped with knowledge, opportunities, and hope.

Pioneer Chairman

Mr. David Ibeh

Pioneer Chairman, Board of Trustees

Mr. David Ibeh serves as the pioneer Chairman of the proposed El-Goshen Foundation. A respected mentor and leader, he provides strategic guidance, governance oversight, and leadership support.

He ensures that the Foundation remains faithful to its mission while pursuing excellence, accountability, and sustainable impact. His experience, integrity, and commitment to human development make him an invaluable leader.
