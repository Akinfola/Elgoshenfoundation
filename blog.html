Featured Campaign

Upcoming October 2026: The Goshen Breast Health Initiative Pilot Outreach

Campaign Theme: “Know Your Breasts. Protect Your Future.”

Join El-Goshen Foundation during Breast Cancer Awareness Month for our first major public campaign targeting 50+ women with free workshops, anatomical model demonstrations, myths & facts Q&A, and clinical referral pathways.

October 2026 Founder Esther Kukoyi Read Full Plan

Key Highlights of the Outreach:

  • 3-Hour Interactive Workshop: Led by qualified healthcare professionals.
  • Breast Self-Awareness Demo: Using approved visual models.
  • Myths vs. Facts Session: Addressing misconceptions around breast changes.
  • Take-Home Educational Packages: Health leaflets & local facility referral details.
