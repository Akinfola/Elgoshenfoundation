Flagship Health Programme

The Goshen Breast Health Initiative (GBHI)

Theme: “Know Your Breasts. Protect Your Future.”

October 2026 Pilot Plan

Background & Purpose

Breast-related conditions remain a significant public health concern. Many women delay seeking medical attention due to fear, myths, misinformation, stigma, financial barriers, or lack of awareness.

While not every breast lump is cancer, every unusual breast change deserves proper medical evaluation. Early detection significantly improves treatment outcomes for breast cancer and helps identify non-cancerous conditions requiring appropriate care.

Inspired by Experience

GBHI was inspired by Founder Esther Kukoyi's personal journey of recurring breast lumps over several years, transforming her gratitude into a mission to ensure no woman faces breast health concerns alone or without information.

The 4 Strategic Pillars of GBHI

1. EDUCATE

Deliver workshops, school talks, market outreach, and faith-based health education.

2. EMPOWER

Equip women with breast self-examination skills, body confidence, and timely presentation awareness.

3. CONNECT

Collaborate with qualified healthcare providers to build direct referral pathways for clinical evaluation.

4. ADVOCATE

Break stigma, dispel myths, and foster supportive family and community dialogues.

Target Beneficiaries of GBHI

Primary Beneficiaries

  • Women aged 15 years and above
  • Female students & young adults
  • Market women & informal sector workers
  • Rural & underserved community women
  • Faith-based women's organizations
  • Persons with disabilities & caregivers

Secondary Beneficiaries

  • Men & family members (encouraging supportive conversations)
  • Teachers & school administrators
  • Community & traditional leaders
  • Youth leaders & peer educators
  • Primary healthcare workers & community health extension workers
Appendix A - Project Proposal

October 2026 Breast Cancer Awareness Month Pilot Outreach

Theme: “Know Your Breasts. Protect Your Future.”

Target Audience 50 Women (Expandable with Sponsorship)
Venue Type Community Centre / Church Hall / PHC Partner
Workshop Duration Approximately 3 Hours

Proposed 3-Hour Workshop Programme Agenda:

  1. Registration & Welcome: Participant check-in and introductory materials.
  2. Opening Remarks: Intro to El-Goshen Foundation by Pioneer Chairman or Founder.
  3. Breast Health Presentation: Interactive session led by a qualified healthcare professional.
  4. Practical Self-Examination Demo: Visual aids & anatomical models demonstration.
  1. Myths & Facts Q&A: Dispelling misconceptions surrounding breast conditions.
  2. Referral Information: Connecting participants with local clinical services.
  3. Feedback & Photos: Participant evaluations, group photo, and closing.
  4. Take-Home Packages: Educational leaflets, contact card & volunteer forms.
